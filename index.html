How to put together a PB&J sandwhich

Ingredients

  • Peanut butter
  • Jelly
  • Two slices of bread

Instructions

  1. Spread one piece of bread with peanut butter
  2. Spread the other piece of bread with jelly
  3. Sandwhich the bread together with spread side inward
  4. Place sandwhich on plate
  5. Cut sandwhich in half
  6. Serve sandwhich immediately
